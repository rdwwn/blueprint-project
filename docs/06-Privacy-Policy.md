# 06 — Privacy Policy

> How The Blueprint Project handles data: minimal, transparent, never sold. Effective: August 2026. Modeled on industry-standard privacy policies (similar in structure to Snowday, Notion, Asana) but written for our specific product and audience.

**Last updated:** August 2026
**Effective date:** August 2026

---

## The short version

We don't collect personal information. We don't require accounts. We don't track you across the web. We don't sell data — we don't have any to sell. We use cookies in the strict technical sense (browser localStorage) to remember your saved programs and preferences. That's it.

If you want the long version, it's below.

---

## 1. Personal Information We Collect

### What we DON'T collect

To be clear about what we don't do:

- We don't require you to create an account
- We don't ask for your name, email, phone, address, or school
- We don't collect your date of birth or grade level
- We don't track you across other websites
- We don't run advertising networks
- We don't sell, rent, or share any personal data — because we don't have any

### What we DO collect (and why)

| Data | Where it lives | Why |
|------|---------------|-----|
| **Search and browse activity** (pages visited, filters used) | Our server logs, retained for 30 days | To understand which programs are popular, fix bugs, and improve the site. Aggregated. Not linked to you as a person. |
| **Saved programs** (when you click the bookmark icon) | Your browser's localStorage | So you can come back to your list. Never leaves your device. |
| **Application status** (saved / applied / accepted) | Your browser's localStorage | So your dashboard shows your progress. Never leaves your device. |
| **Custom dates** (exams, interviews you add) | Your browser's localStorage | So your calendar includes your personal dates. Never leaves your device. |
| **Program reviews you write** (rating, text, optional name/grade) | Your browser's localStorage | So other students can read your review. Never leaves your device unless you explicitly post it. |
| **Program flags you submit** (via email) | Our email inbox (Gmail) | So the Research Lead can fix the issue. |
| **Error reports** | Our hosting (Vercel) and error tracking (if we add it) | To fix bugs. |
| **Server logs** (IP address, user agent, timestamp) | Vercel logs, retained for 30 days | To detect abuse and debug issues. |

When we talk about "Personal Information" in this Privacy Policy, we are talking about anything that could identify you. The only thing that can identify you is your IP address, which is in our server logs for 30 days, and your name/grade if you choose to include it in a public review.

### Third-party services that may collect data when you visit our site

- **Mapbox** — When you view the map, Mapbox may set its own cookies or localStorage items to remember your map preferences (zoom, pan, locale). Mapbox's privacy practices are governed by [their own privacy policy](https://www.mapbox.com/legal/privacy). We do not have access to the data Mapbox collects.
- **Unsplash** — Cover images on this site are loaded from images.unsplash.com. Unsplash may log your IP address for the purpose of serving images. See [Unsplash's privacy policy](https://unsplash.com/privacy).
- **Vercel** — Our site is hosted on Vercel. Vercel collects server logs (IP, user agent) for the purpose of operating the service. See [Vercel's privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## 2. How We Use Your Personal Information

We use the limited information we collect to:

- **Operate the service.** Serve the website, run the map, load program data.
- **Improve the service.** Understand which programs are popular. Fix bugs. Respond to flagged content.
- **Communicate with you (only if you reach out first).** When you email us, we reply.
- **Detect and prevent abuse.** Identify bots, scraping, denial-of-service attacks.

We do NOT use your information to:

- Target you with advertising
- Build a profile of you
- Sell to data brokers
- Share with third parties for any purpose

---

## 3. Sharing Your Personal Information

We don't sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your Personal Information to third parties. Period.

We may share your Personal Information only in these limited circumstances:

- **Service providers who help us run the site.** Our hosting provider (Vercel) and our map provider (Mapbox) see technical data (IP, user agent) to deliver the service. They are bound by their own privacy policies and we choose providers with strong privacy practices.
- **When required by law.** If a court orders us to disclose information, or if we're required to do so by a valid subpoena. We've never received one. If we did, we'd fight it.
- **To protect our rights or safety.** If we believe disclosure is necessary to investigate, prevent, or take action regarding illegal activities, suspected fraud, or violations of our Terms of Service.
- **With your consent.** If you explicitly authorize us to share something.

We do not use Google Analytics or any third-party analytics that follow you across the web. We may use privacy-respecting aggregate analytics (e.g., Plausible, Fathom) in the future. If we do, this policy will be updated.

## 4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

**Short version: We don't use cookies. We use localStorage, which is similar but stays on your device.**

For the long version, see `07-Cookies-Policy.md`.

In summary:

- We do not set cookies
- We do not use third-party tracking pixels
- We do use browser localStorage to remember your saved programs, custom dates, and reviews — this data never leaves your device
- You can clear localStorage at any time in your browser settings; this will erase your saved data on this device
- The map (powered by Mapbox) does not set tracking cookies on our site

## 7. Your Rights

You have the following rights with respect to your personal information:

### For everyone, everywhere

- **Right to access.** You can see all the data we have about you by emailing us. (Spoiler: we probably don't have any.)
- **Right to delete.** You can ask us to delete any data we have about you. We will do so within 30 days.
- **Right to correct.** You can ask us to correct inaccurate data.
- **Right to export.** You can ask for a copy of any data we have about you in a portable format.

### For users in the EU / UK / EEA / Switzerland / UK

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR:

- **Right to restrict processing.** You can ask us to limit how we use your data.
- **Right to object.** You can object to certain types of processing.
- **Right to data portability.** You can receive your data in a machine-readable format.
- **Right to lodge a complaint.** You can complain to your local data protection authority.
- **Right to withdraw consent.** If we ever rely on consent (we currently don't), you can withdraw it at any time.

We process your information only on the following legal bases under GDPR:
- **Legitimate interest** (to operate and improve the service)
- **Legal obligation** (to comply with applicable laws)
- **Consent** (only if you opt in to something new, like a newsletter)

We do not transfer your data outside of your country without appropriate safeguards. We use Vercel (US-based) and Mapbox (US-based). For EEA users, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses for any cross-border transfers.

### For users in California

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

- You have the right to know what personal information is collected, used, shared, or sold
- You have the right to delete personal information
- You have the right to opt out of the sale of personal information (we don't sell)
- You have the right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

### For users in other jurisdictions

We extend the same rights listed above to all users, regardless of jurisdiction. If your local law provides additional rights, we honor those too.

### How to exercise your rights

Email **contact@blueprintproject.app** with the subject "PRIVACY REQUEST." We'll respond within 30 days.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain different categories of data for different periods:

| Data | Retention |
|------|-----------|
| **Server logs** (IP, user agent) | 30 days, then deleted |
| **Email correspondence** | Retained as long as needed; deleted on request |
| **Aggregated analytics** (page views, etc.) | Indefinitely (no personal identifiers) |
| **Your localStorage data** (saved programs, custom dates, reviews) | Until you clear your browser data or use the "Reset" feature on the dashboard |
| **Program flags** (when you email us) | Until resolved; archived after |
| **Comments and reviews on the site** | Until you delete them; in localStorage so only you can see them by default |

When our service is discontinued, we will delete all server-side data within 90 days. You will have 30 days' notice to export your localStorage data.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Our audience includes minors, including children under 13. We are committed to protecting children's privacy.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. We do not require accounts, do not use tracking cookies, and do not build advertising profiles of any user. The Blueprint Project is designed to be usable by children under 13 without providing personal information.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information (for example, a name in a public review), contact us at contact@blueprintproject.app with subject "COPPA" and we will delete that information within 5 business days. We will not knowingly retain such information.

We do not use any personal information collected from any user, child or adult, for behavioral advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose unrelated to operating the Service.

## 11. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information:

- **HTTPS everywhere.** All traffic is encrypted in transit.
- **No server-side user data.** The only personal data on our servers is in server logs and email inbox, both with access controls.
- **Regular security reviews.** The codebase is open to public review.
- **Vercel and Mapbox** are SOC 2 compliant providers.

No method of transmission over the Internet, however, is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

If you discover a security vulnerability, please email **contact@blueprintproject.app** with "SECURITY" in the subject. We will respond within 48 hours.

## 12. Your Choices and Controls

- **Clear your localStorage data.** Visit your browser settings → Site settings → Clear data. This will erase your saved programs, custom dates, and reviews on that device.
- **Use the in-app Reset.** On the Dashboard, click "Clear all saved programs" or "Reset all data" in Settings.
- **Block JavaScript.** Our site requires JavaScript. Without it, the site won't work.
- **Use a privacy-focused browser.** We support any modern browser.
- **Contact us.** For anything else, email contact@blueprintproject.app.
