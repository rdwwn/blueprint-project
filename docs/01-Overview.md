# 01 — Overview

> The short version of what The Blueprint Project is, who it's for, and what it does. Read this first.

---

## What it is

The Blueprint Project is a **free, hand-verified directory of internships, scholarships, competitions, and research programs for high school students**. It is the only place a 9th–12th grader needs to look to find every serious opportunity they're eligible for, ranked by relevance, with real deadlines and a one-click path to the official application.

We are **not** an application portal. We do not run programs, take applications, or take a cut. We are a discovery and organization layer that helps students find what's out there and apply on the program's own site.

## Why it exists

High-achieving high schoolers who land at top colleges and selective programs almost always start earlier than their peers. They get there because someone — a parent, a teacher, a counselor, a paid consultant — told them which opportunities to look at and when to apply.

That information is locked behind:

- **Incomplete databases** that only list the biggest programs and miss regional/local scholarships
- **Paid consulting** ($1,000+ for college admissions advising, $200+ per hour for college counseling)
- **Family networks** — most students at top schools have parents who went to top schools

Blueprint is the free, public-good version. The mission: **make the same opportunity awareness available to every high schooler, regardless of family income, zip code, or social capital.**

## Who it's for

| Audience | What they get |
|----------|---------------|
| **High school students (grades 9–12)** | Search 2,400+ programs; save what they care about; track deadlines; get matched via the Find Your Path quiz |
| **Parents and guardians** | A reliable list of verified, age-appropriate opportunities; deadline calendar for their kid's grade |
| **School counselors and educators** | A free curriculum-aligned resource; bulk-exportable lists for grade-level advising |
| **Program organizers** | Free distribution; user feedback loop; corrected dead links reported back to them |

## What's in the database (as of August 2026)

- **2,418** verified opportunities
- **50+** fields (CS & Engineering, Medicine & Health, Arts, Business, Space, Journalism, etc.)
- **Programs from 30+ countries** — primarily US, with growing international coverage
- **Every category a high schooler applies to:**
  - Summer programs (MITES, RSI, COSMOS, Google CSSI)
  - Paid internships (NASA OSTEM, NIH, Jane Street)
  - Scholarships (QuestBridge, Coca-Cola, Jack Kent Cooke)
  - Competitions (FIRST, Science Olympiad, Regeneron ISEF)
  - Research programs (high school science, humanities, computational)
  - School-year and ongoing opportunities

## What it does (features)

1. **Search & filter** — by field, category, location, cost, grade, season, deadline window, tags
2. **Map view** — US and World view; click pins to see programs; toggle between marker styles
3. **Compare** — side-by-side comparison of up to 4 programs
4. **Save & dashboard** — save programs, mark status (saved / applied / accepted), get a deadline calendar
5. **Find Your Path** — 60-second quiz that recommends programs based on interests, grade, and goals
6. **Contribute** — flag wrong info, share feedback, build a public list
7. **Insights & guides** — blog, resources library, success stories, college comparison

## What it doesn't do (yet)

- **Accounts / sign-up.** Everything is local-first. Your data lives in your browser. We do not require an account, and we do not store your personal information on our servers.
- **Application submission.** Applications happen on the program's own site. Blueprint is discovery only.
- **Admissions consulting.** We list programs. We don't advise students on essays, interviews, or which programs to "chase."
- **Mobile app.** Mobile web is excellent. A native app is a future phase.

## How it stays free

- **No ads.** We don't run advertising. We don't sell user data.
- **No paid tiers.** Every feature is free for every user.
- **No "featured" listings.** Programs are ranked by relevance and deadline, not by who paid.
- **Operational costs** are small (~$30/month for hosting + Mapbox free tier). Founder covers them. Future: grant funding, optional donor-supported "Fellowship" tier with no perks attached.
